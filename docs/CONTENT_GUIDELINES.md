# Content Guidelines

## Language

**Simple Indian English.** Write as you would speak to a customer face-to-face.

Target audience: everyday Kerala households.
Reading level: comfortable for someone reading English as a second language.

## Writing principles

✅ **Do:**
- Short sentences (under 20 words when possible)
- Active voice
- Simple, common words
- One idea per paragraph
- Clear call-to-action

❌ **Don't:**
- Corporate marketing language
- Complicated vocabulary
- Excessive adjectives
- Long paragraphs
- AI-sounding promotional sentences

## Acceptable writing examples

```
Good food for every day.
Explore our products.
Made for everyday cleaning.
Find the right product for your home.
Have a question? Contact us.
Good products at fair prices.
Available in Kerala and nearby states.
```

## Prohibited claims

Do not write any of the following unless Epso provides verified evidence:

- 100% natural
- Chemical-free
- Best in India
- No. 1 brand
- Certified quality (without naming the certification)
- Lab-tested
- Clinically proven
- Ayurvedic
- Organic (without certification)
- Safe for babies / children (without safety data)

## Placeholder text conventions

Use these when actual content is not yet available:

```
📝 This content is a placeholder. Update with [description] in Sanity CMS.
[To be updated]
[Company information coming soon]
[Product description to be added]
```

Always make placeholders obvious. Never publish a page that looks complete but contains fabricated information.

## Product descriptions

### Short description (product cards, 1–2 sentences)
- State what the product is.
- State who it is for or when to use it.
- Maximum 200 characters.

**Good:**
> Kerala-style spiced peanut mixture. Good for snacking at home or as a tea-time snack.

**Bad:**
> Experience the unparalleled taste of our premium artisanal Kerala peanut mixture, crafted with 100% natural ingredients and time-honoured traditional recipes!

### Full description (product page)
- 2–4 short paragraphs.
- What is it? Who is it for? How is it used?
- Do not repeat the same information from the short description word for word.

## Category descriptions

- 1–2 sentences.
- Describe the range of products in the category.
- Help users understand what they will find.

**Good:**
> Dishwashing liquids, floor cleaners and household cleaning products for everyday use.

**Bad:**
> Discover our revolutionary range of innovative home care solutions engineered for superior cleaning performance.

## About page

- Do not invent company history.
- Do not invent founding dates.
- Do not invent manufacturing locations.
- Do not invent certifications.
- Use `[To be updated]` for all unknown business information.

## Blog articles

- Practical, helpful topics relevant to Epso's products.
- Examples: home cleaning tips, snack ideas, storage advice.
- 400–800 words per article.
- Simple, numbered or bulleted instructions.
- Do not publish SEO-stuffed, low-quality articles.

## Sanity CMS field guidance

| Field | Note |
|-------|------|
| Product name | Official product name. No marketing words. |
| Short description | 1–2 plain sentences. Max 200 characters. |
| Ingredients | Factual ingredient list. No health claims. |
| Usage instructions | Simple steps. No unverified safety claims. |
| SEO title | Include product name. Under 60 characters. |
| SEO description | Describe the product plainly. Under 160 characters. |
| Alt text | Describe what is in the image. |
| Blog excerpt | Plain summary of the article. Under 300 characters. |

## Translation preparation

The website is built to support multiple languages.
Current language: English (default).
Planned: Malayalam.

When translating:
- Maintain the same plain-language style.
- Do not translate placeholder text.
- Keep the same tone — do not use overly formal Malayalam.
- Translated pages will use the same Sanity content with language-specific fields.
